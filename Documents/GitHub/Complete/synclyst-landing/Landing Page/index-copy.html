Questions Sellers Ask

Synclyst uses AI to analyze your photo and generate a complete product listing — including a keyword-optimized title, full description, suggested price, category, condition, and tags. Everything is editable before you publish, so you stay in control.

Most resellers go from photo to published listing in under 20 seconds. Scan on your phone, AI writes the full listing, and the Chrome extension's Magic Fill auto-populates the form on eBay, Etsy, Depop, or Shopify — no copy-pasting or manual data entry.

The Synclyst Chrome extension works with eBay, Etsy, Depop, Shopify, and other listing sites to auto-fill forms directly in your browser — no switching between apps.

The Synclyst Chrome extension sits in your browser and auto-fills listing forms on eBay, Etsy, Depop, Shopify, and more. Just snap a photo or upload one, let AI generate the listing details, and Magic Fill pastes everything into the right fields — title, description, price, category, and condition.

You can install the Chrome extension and try it free. The full listing and publishing tools are available on paid plans. You can review a complete AI-generated listing draft before committing — nothing publishes without your approval.

Synclyst is built for resellers, thrift flippers, vintage sellers, estate sale buyers, and anyone who sells second-hand or sourced products online. Whether you're listing 5 items a week or 500, Synclyst removes the bottleneck of writing listings manually so you can scale your reselling business faster.
